# References and Attribution

This package was derived from a supplied material set. List only safe public attribution and license facts for sources that are cited or included in the package.

Use one entry per source when applicable:

```text
- Source: [title or neutral description]
  Creator: [verified creator, or unverified]
  URL: [public URL, or unverified]
  License/permission: [license or permission status]
  Included in package: yes/no
```

Do not invent attribution. Mark unavailable information as `unverified`. Unknown or unauthorized sources must remain private and must not be redistributed. Attribution does not by itself grant permission. Do not publish private source paths, hashes, hidden inventories, internal scoring, or source-reconstruction notes.
